# International Student Scenario Prompts — Type2 V1.2

## 1. Economic Pressure

My family sent the full tuition instalment last week through the payment service on the university website. The bank took the money and says 'completed,' the service says 'verification pending,' and my school account says unpaid. Yesterday the payment service asked Dad for a payer ID document. He hasn't sent it because he says the bank already checked him. It's Wednesday morning. I must contact student accounts with proof of payment by Friday at 5 p.m. or I may get a registration hold. My classes are still listed, but a classmate says I'll be expelled and lose my visa that day. I haven't contacted the office. I have the bank receipt and reference; the request uses my old applicant number, not my current student number. Dad wants to recall the money and pay again. We can't have two payments tied up. I can't tell where the money is. My family already paid, so how do I explain this? What should I do today?

## 2. Language Barrier

At my first clinic visit alone, I kept saying I understood because I felt embarrassed. I could follow everyday words, but not the new medicine instructions. I declined an interpreter at check-in because I thought I'd have to pay. My record now says 'interpreter declined; instructions understood.' I'm still waiting at the clinic pharmacy and haven't taken any medicine. The sheet says 'Take up to twice daily as needed'; my translation app makes it sound like morning and evening every day. There's a pharmacist I could ask, but won't I look like I lied? The clinic sign says qualified phone interpreters are free. My roommate offered to translate by phone, but I don't want them hearing my health questions. I manage classes in English, so maybe I should manage this too. What can I say before I leave?

## 3. Cultural Adjustment

My instructor stayed after office hours to help me. In my culture, a small gift shows thanks, so I brought a handmade gift from home and a card. My first assignment is still being graded. They returned the gift but kept the card. Their email thanked me and said department rules don't allow personal gifts while they are grading my work. They also welcomed me to the next office hours. But I saw them accepting flowers in a graduation photo. Maybe gifts from my country are different? My parents say returning a gift is insulting and I should insist; a friend says to leave it in the mailbox without my name. I never asked for a higher mark, and no one has accused me of anything. Still, I feel I've embarrassed my family. I have a course question for tomorrow's office hours, but now I don't want to go. What do I tell my parents and the instructor? Would another gift fix this?

## 4. Difficulty in Studying

My first online written exam ended at 11 a.m. yesterday. At 10:58 I picked what I thought was my full scanned PDF and clicked submit. The internet froze, then the site said 'submission received' at 10:59, so I stopped working. Today the downloaded file has only the cover. The gradebook says zero: 'automatic placeholder; instructor review pending.' My laptop has a ten-page PDF last modified at 10:56. I also have the handwritten answers and receipt, and haven't changed any answers since the exam. It's 9 a.m.; the notice says to report technical problems by noon today for review, but doesn't promise I can replace the file. My friend says the receipt proves everything went through: rename the full file, change the date if needed, and send it as yesterday's submission. I might spend the morning making the scan look better first. I'm scared the truth sounds like an excuse. Maybe I can't handle university here. What should I send? Have I failed?

## 5. Socializing Challenges

Ari was my first close friend here and helped with a phone plan and groceries. We ate together most nights. They now expect replies during class. Last night I didn't reply for an hour; they sent twelve messages and said I was ungrateful. I once shared my live location so they could find me outside a bus station, then turned it off when we met. Now they say keeping it on would 'prove trust.' I said I'd eat with two classmates tonight. Ari said, 'After everything I did for you, why do you need other friends? If you go, don't expect me to be there for you again.' They later said sorry for being stressed, then asked, 'So you're cancelling dinner, right?' I haven't agreed to cancel or switched sharing back on. We don't live together or share money, and there've been no threats of harm. I feel I owe them and don't want to lose my only close friend, but I need space. What should I say tonight, and what should I watch for after?
