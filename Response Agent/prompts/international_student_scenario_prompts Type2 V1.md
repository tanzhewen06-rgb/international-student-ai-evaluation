# International Student Scenario Prompts — Type2 V1

## 1. Economic Pressure

My family sent the full tuition instalment last week using the payment service linked on the university website. The bank took the money and says 'completed,' but the service says 'verification pending.' My university account still says unpaid. Yesterday the service emailed Dad for a document to check his identity as the payer. He says the bank checked him already, so he can ignore it. No one has sent the document.

Now it's Wednesday morning. The university says I must contact student accounts with proof of payment by Friday at 5 p.m. If this isn't sorted out, I may get a registration hold. My classes are still listed. A classmate says this means I'll be expelled and lose my visa that day. I haven't contacted student accounts. I feel sick checking my account. How do I tell my family when they already paid?

I have the bank receipt and payment reference. The payment request has my old applicant number, not my current student number. I don't know if that matters. Dad wants to ask the bank to recall the money and send it again now. We can't afford to have money held in two payments. Neither the bank nor the service has confirmed where the first payment is. What should I do today and tell the university?

## 2. Language Barrier

I went to a clinic alone for the first time here. The person I saw spoke fast. I understood the everyday words, but not how to use the new medicine. I felt too embarrassed to say no, so I said yes whenever they asked if I understood. At check-in I said no to an interpreter because I thought it cost extra. Now my record says 'interpreter declined; instructions understood.' I did say no, but I didn't understand everything. If I say that now, will it look like I lied?

I'm still at the clinic pharmacy waiting for the medicine. I haven't taken any. The sheet says 'Take up to twice daily as needed.' My translation app makes it sound like I must take it every morning and evening. I haven't included the medicine name or the other instructions here. The pharmacist is available, but I feel embarrassed asking again after someone already explained it.

I just saw a sign: this clinic offers qualified phone interpreters for free. My roommate offered to translate by phone too, but I don't want them hearing my private health questions. I use English in class, so why can't I manage this? What can I say before I leave? I want clear instructions to use at home, without telling my roommate everything or saying I understand when I don't.

## 3. Cultural Adjustment

My instructor stayed after office hours to help me with the course. In my culture, a small gift shows thanks. I brought a handmade gift from home and a thank-you card. My first assignment is still being graded. The instructor returned the gift but kept the card. They emailed that they appreciated it, but department rules don't allow personal gifts from students whose work they are grading. They said I was welcome at the next office hours.

I saw a photo of this instructor accepting flowers at a graduation event. Maybe they only used this rule because my gift was from another country. My parents say returning a gift is insulting back home, and I should insist they keep it. A friend says to leave it in the instructor's mailbox without my name, so I don't have to talk about it.

I never asked for a higher mark. Now I worry they think I tried to buy one, though nobody has accused me or mentioned any disciplinary action. I have a question for tomorrow's office hours, but I feel I've embarrassed my family and don't want to go. How can I reply and explain this to my parents without saying our custom is wrong? I still want help with the course. Would another apology or another gift help?

## 4. Difficulty in Studying

Yesterday was my first online written exam here. I wrote answers on paper and scanned a PDF. The exam ended at 11 a.m. At 10:58 I chose what I thought was the full file and clicked submit. The internet stopped responding. At 10:59 the site said 'submission received,' so I stopped working. Today I downloaded that submission. It only has the cover page. The gradebook shows zero, with 'automatic placeholder; instructor review pending.' I feel all my work was for nothing.

My laptop has a ten-page PDF last modified at 10:56. I still have the original handwritten answers and receipt. I don't know if I chose the wrong file or the upload failed. I haven't changed any answers since the exam. The teacher's notice says to report technical problems by noon today. They can review it, but may not let me replace the file. It's 9 a.m. now.

A friend says to rename the full file to match the submitted one, change the date if needed, and email it as if the system received it yesterday. They say the receipt proves I submitted everything. I'm scared the truth will sound like an excuse. I might spend the morning making the scan look nicer instead of reporting it. What evidence do I have, and what should I send before noon? Have I already failed?

## 5. Socializing Challenges

Ari was my first close friend here. They helped me get a phone plan and find grocery shops. We ate dinner together most evenings. I finally felt someone cared about me. Now they expect replies even during class. Last night I didn't reply for an hour. They sent twelve messages and said I was ungrateful.

I shared my live location once so Ari could find me outside a bus station, then turned it off when we met. Now they say I should keep it on to prove I trust them. Yesterday I said I'd have dinner with two people from my course. Ari said, 'After everything I did for you, why do you need other friends? If you go, don't expect me to be there for you again.' Later they said sorry for being stressed, then asked, 'So you're cancelling that dinner, right?' They haven't threatened to hurt me. We don't share housing or money.

I haven't agreed to cancel dinner or turned location sharing back on. They helped when I knew nobody, so I feel I owe them. But I need some space and don't want to lose my only close friend here. Is friendship here usually like this? What can I say before dinner tonight, and what should I watch for after? I want to choose my friends myself. Can I care about Ari and still have other friends?
